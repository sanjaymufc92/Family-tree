Hatti map

Badaga hattis across the Nilgiris, coloured by seemai. Big circles are your family's villages, sized by how many people come from each; lines show marriages between them. Drag to move, and tap a hatti to see its name.

Hatti locations are post office points from India Post data, so each dot is near the hatti, not exact. District outline: Census 2011 (DataMeet, CC BY 2.5 IN).

Marriage links

Each circle is a village, coloured by its seemai and sized by how many family members come from it. Lines show marriages between villages. Tap a village to see its people.
